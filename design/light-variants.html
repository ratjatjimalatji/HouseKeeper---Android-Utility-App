HouseKeeper: light mode for layout B

B's layout with the changes you asked for: C's utility icon tiles on Home, bar charts for daily usage, and the 7-day, month-end usage, month-end cost and confidence figures on Reports. The screens are identical across the three themes; only the colours change.

1 · Amber Daylight

B's dark-mode amber on a cool white background. The closest match to the bold, bright Scan button you liked.

Notes

  • Amber is used only as a fill with dark text on it. Amber text on white fails contrast, so text uses a darker amber-brown.
  • Pairs naturally with B's dark theme as the night version.
  • Risk: amber is also the electricity colour, so the brand and electricity can blur together.

2 · Slate Brand

Uses the app's existing slate blue as the primary colour. Continuity with today's House Check.

Notes

  • Brand colour is separate from all three utility colours, so amber always means electricity.
  • White text on slate passes contrast comfortably.
  • Less bright than 1. The Scan button stands out by shape and position more than by colour.

3 · Warm Mint

Warm paper background with a vivid mint-green primary. Fresh and friendly, and it reads as "savings".

Notes

  • Green doesn't collide with any utility colour and fits the money-saving story.
  • The warm background keeps a hint of option C's softer feel.
  • Green is also used for "normal / OK" states, so I've kept those a darker shade.
